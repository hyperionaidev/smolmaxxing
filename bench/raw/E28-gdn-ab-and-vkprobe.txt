> Explain in three sentences why the sky is blue.
Sunlight, which is composed of all visible colors, travels through the atmosphere and hits gas molecules called nitrogen and oxygen. These molecules absorb the longer wavelengths of light, such as red and orange, while scattering the shorter wavelengths, specifically blue light, in all directions. This scattering effect, known as Rayleigh scattering, is most intense for blue light, making it the dominant color we see when looking up
